# Surface brief: public landing page

Scope: the public site's front door (/), sharing its world with every public page, sign-in and the signed-in app. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: solo founders and small teams deciding whether to hand real work to AI staff; the action is joining the beta.
Constraints: no testimonials, logos or usage numbers; WCAG 2.2 AA gated by check:contrast; copy unchanged unless asked.
Direction: user asked for vivid yet calm, fresh, charming, cute - no neon, no brown. Roll 7be65774 assigned grounded candidate 3 (sticky-note studio); the user chose it over the summer-morning-sky pick. Challengers (suminagashi, phosphor terminal, Japanese high-density, Art Paul program, rain city, streetwear) declined as too dark or dense for "calm". Kept from Japanese high-density: small coloured tabs heading each module. Kept from the Art Paul program: the Desker mark hidden as a stamp on the lead note.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Your staff leave you notes. The page is a desk of bright sticky notes from named agents around the product, each waiting for your yes; it refuses the dark AI launch page and the grey SaaS grid.
OWN-WORLD: crisp white paper with a faint blue dot grid; sticky-note fills in lemon, sky, mint, coral, lilac; white rounded cards with hairlines; a strip of translucent washi tape on note cards; a lemon highlighter swipe under key words; one strong colour, a clear ink blue, for actions and links; navy ink text; a friendly bold rounded grotesk for display, a hand face only for scribbles on notes.
STORY: see what the agents do, see notes waiting for approval, see the limits, join the beta.
FIRST VIEWPORT: split: on the left the handwritten (Caveat) headline with one highlighter stroke, subhead, the Caveat "Join the beta" pill and a white pill, and the five staff faces; on the right a large live work desk (staff rail + each agent's real output), with two notes stuck to its top and bottom edges; one compact note above the desk on phones.
FORM: grounded candidate 3 of 7, seed 7be65774.
